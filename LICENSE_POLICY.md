### Scope and contributions

- Contributors retain copyright in their contributions. This policy requires
  no copyright assignment and asserts no exclusive ownership of every file.
- Preserve existing copyright, license, and attribution notices. Third-party
  code, build tools, assets, libraries, and separately acquired models retain
  their applicable terms. The root LICENSE does not relicense them.
- Changes to third-party material must respect its upstream terms; identify
  its source and the modification.
- New original source files should carry SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-only.
  Do not replace third-party or legacy notices with blanket AGPL headers.
- For legacy files with new original changes, preserve earlier notices and
  identify the changes and this policy. Do not offer the new changes under an
  Apache/AGPL choice unless the contributor authorizes that extra permission.
- Check compatibility and distribution obligations for the actual combined
  program and dependencies; distribute required license texts and notices.

### Practical effect

AGPL permits commercial use. Source availability follows its distribution
conditions and, for a modified version supporting remote network interaction,
the offer of Corresponding Source in section 13. It does not require publication
of every private local modification, impose automatic royalties, prevent use of
the historical Apache version, or grant exclusive rights to names, logos, or
external model weights.

This policy adds no custom noncommercial restriction or mandatory payment.
License texts control their respective material; this document explains scope
and does not amend those texts.

hanno come licenza predefinita **AGPL-3.0-only**, salvo avvisi specifici
applicabili. I contributori mantengono il proprio copyright.

I permessi Apache-2.0 già concessi fino al commit 37bf1f1afd311cac8ddb56465b5cf67e8865e4c1 restano validi.
Il materiale precedente invariato resta Apache; le modifiche originali nuove
seguono la nuova regola senza cancellare i diritti sulle parti precedenti.
Licenza e NOTICE storici sono conservati nella cartella LICENSES.

Licenze e attribuzioni di codice esterno, strumenti di build, asset, librerie e
modelli restano applicabili e non vanno sovrascritte con AGPL.

AGPL permette l'uso commerciale. La disponibilità del sorgente segue le sue
condizioni di distribuzione e, per versioni modificate accessibili via rete,
la sezione 13. Non impone royalty, non blocca l'uso delle versioni Apache
precedenti e non crea diritti esclusivi su marchi o materiali di terzi.
